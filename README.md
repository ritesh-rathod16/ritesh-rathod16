## 💡 Featured Projects

### 🧠 Vidhara — AI Contract Explainer

A privacy-first product concept designed to make complex contracts easier to understand.

**Key planned capabilities:**
- 📄 Upload PDF, DOCX, and TXT documents
- 📝 Generate plain-language contract summaries
- ⚠️ Highlight potential risks and important clauses
- 📅 Extract obligations, deadlines, and financial terms
- 🔍 Explore individual clauses
- 💬 Ask document-grounded follow-up questions
- 🌐 Support English, Hindi, and Marathi
- 🔒 Prioritize document privacy, deletion, and limited retention

**Design principle:** Explain contractual language without pretending to replace a qualified legal professional.

**Status:** Product requirements and architecture planning.
